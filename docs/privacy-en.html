Summary

This app helps you trace drawings by overlaying a reference image on paper or your screen. No account is required. We do not ask for email or password, and we do not sell your personal data.

Data we collect and how we use it

  • Camera: only when you use “draw with camera” mode, to show a live preview on your device. We do not record or upload video to our servers.
  • Gallery images: images you choose are processed on your phone to display on the canvas. We do not upload them to our servers.
  • Online templates: when you pick a library template, the app downloads the image from our catalog (hosted on Supabase) and may cache it locally.
  • Internet: required to load templates and display ads.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): the app shows banners and interstitials. Google and its partners may collect your device advertising ID, IP address, ad interaction data, and technical device data to measure and personalize ads, per Google’s Privacy Policy. You can limit ad personalization in Android ad settings or your Google account.

Data we do not collect

  • Name, email, phone number, or login credentials
  • Contacts, SMS, GPS location, or microphone
  • Gallery content sent to our servers

Sharing with third parties

  • Google AdMob / Google Play Services — ads and Google services on the device.
  • Supabase — hosting of the public template catalog (image URLs). We do not send personally identifiable data to Supabase from the app.

We do not sell or rent your personal data.

Retention and deletion

Downloaded templates may remain in your phone’s cache. You can remove them by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android Settings. Data processed by Google AdMob is governed by Google’s policies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us to request deletion.

Security

Catalog connections use HTTPS. Gallery image processing happens locally on the device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page. The “last updated” date reflects the current version. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support email listed on the AR Draw: Easy Sketching Google Play store listing.
