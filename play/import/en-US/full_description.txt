AR Draw: Easy Sketching helps you copy any drawing by overlaying a reference image on paper.

HOW IT WORKS
• Choose camera mode (tripod or propped phone) or screen mode (paper on the phone).
• Pick a library template or a photo from your gallery.
• Adjust opacity, zoom, and rotation, lock the image, and draw.

FEATURES
• No account or sign-in required
• Online template library (free and premium)
• Use your own images
• Zoom, opacity, 90° rotation, and position lock
• Front or back camera and flash
• Collapsible tools panel

ADS
The app includes Google AdMob banners and interstitials. Premium templates and gallery use may show a full-screen ad before opening the canvas.

REQUIREMENTS
Android 8.0 or later.
